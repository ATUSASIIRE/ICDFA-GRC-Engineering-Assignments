# Monthly Security Awareness Calendar

## January: Baseline Assessment

**Theme:** Understand the organisation's current human-risk position.

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| **Primary audience** | All employees |
| **Owner** | Security Manager and HR Manager |
| **Activities** | Employee awareness questionnaire; baseline phishing simulation; review of incident reports, previous training records, password-policy compliance, and MFA adoption |
| **Communication** | Programme launch message from Executive Management |
| **Measurement** | Phishing click and reporting rates, knowledge score, existing MFA adoption, previous training participation, and incident-reporting volume |
| **Deliverable** | Approved Month 1 baseline report |

**Success checkpoint:** Baseline data is documented without collecting employees' actual passwords.

---

## February: Phishing and Invoice-Fraud Prevention

**Theme:** Stop fraudulent messages and payment requests before they cause loss.

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| **Primary audience** | Head Office Staff and Managers |
| **Owner** | Security Manager |
| **Activities** | Short awareness session; invoice-verification demonstration; suspicious-email examples; guidance on independent payment confirmation |
| **Communication** | Email tip sheet on sender checks, urgency cues, links, attachments, and bank-detail changes |
| **Measurement** | Short knowledge assessment and participation rate |
| **Deliverable** | Phishing and invoice-fraud awareness pack |

**Expected behaviour:** Employees verify unusual payment instructions through a separate trusted channel and report suspicious requests before acting.

---

## March: Password Security and MFA

**Theme:** Protect accounts from credential reuse and unauthorised access.

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| **Primary audience** | All employees |
| **Owner** | IT Support Team |
| **Activities** | Password-security briefing; MFA enrolment support; guidance on unique credentials and prohibited credential sharing |
| **Communication** | Password and MFA reminder campaign |
| **Measurement** | Monthly MFA adoption rate and support-request trends |
| **Deliverable** | MFA adoption progress report |

**Target checkpoint:** Progress toward **95% MFA adoption** among eligible employees by the end of Month 6.

---

## April: First Phishing Simulation and Q1 Review

**Theme:** Test awareness and identify early learning gaps.

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| **Primary audience** | All employees |
| **Owner** | Security Manager |
| **Activities** | Controlled phishing simulation using a realistic supplier-invoice scenario; supportive feedback for affected employees; Q1 programme review |
| **Communication** | Post-simulation learning message without public blame or embarrassment |
| **Measurement** | Email opening, link clicking, information submission, and correct reporting rates |
| **Deliverable** | Q1 performance and corrective-action report |

**Governance checkpoint:** Assign owners and target dates to all Q1 corrective actions.

---

## May: Data Protection and Secure Information Sharing

**Theme:** Handle regulated customer information safely.

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| **Primary audience** | Head Office Staff and Remote Sales Staff |
| **Owner** | Security Manager |
| **Activities** | Secure-sharing demonstration; authorised-recipient checks; approved storage and transmission methods; accidental-disclosure reporting |
| **Communication** | Data-handling checklist |
| **Measurement** | Knowledge assessment and participation rate |
| **Deliverable** | Secure information-handling guidance |

**Expected behaviour:** Employees use approved systems, confirm recipients, limit access, and report accidental disclosure immediately.

---

## June: Physical Security and Device Protection

**Theme:** Protect facilities, devices, and operational records.

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| **Primary audience** | Warehouse Staff |
| **Owner** | Department Managers with IT Support |
| **Activities** | Secure-device briefing; physical access awareness; protection of delivery records; lost-device reporting exercise |
| **Communication** | Posters in warehouses and shared areas |
| **Measurement** | Participation, knowledge score, and device-incident reporting |
| **Deliverable** | Warehouse security-awareness summary |

**Midyear checkpoint:** Confirm whether eligible-account MFA adoption has reached **95%** and document remediation where it has not.

---

## July: Second Phishing Simulation and Q2 Review

**Theme:** Measure improvement using a different business scenario.

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| **Primary audience** | All employees |
| **Owner** | Security Manager |
| **Activities** | Controlled delivery-notification or password-reset simulation; targeted refresher support; Q2 review |
| **Communication** | Warning-sign recap and reporting instructions |
| **Measurement** | Click, submission, and reporting rates compared with January and April |
| **Deliverable** | Q2 trend analysis and corrective-action tracker |

**Success checkpoint:** Simulation results show a downward click trend and an upward reporting trend.

---

## August: Secure Remote Working and Access Management

**Theme:** Protect company and customer information outside the office.

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| **Primary audience** | Remote Sales Staff |
| **Owner** | IT Support Team |
| **Activities** | Secure remote-access session; MFA follow-up; safe internet-use guidance; secure customer-information sharing |
| **Communication** | Remote-working security checklist |
| **Measurement** | MFA adoption, knowledge score, and remote-access support trends |
| **Deliverable** | Remote-work security status report |

**Expected behaviour:** Remote employees use approved access methods, MFA, trusted networks, and authorised information-sharing channels.

---

## September: Security Incident Reporting

**Theme:** Make reporting simple, prompt, confidential, and no-blame.

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| **Primary audience** | All employees |
| **Owner** | Security Manager |
| **Activities** | Reporting-process demonstration; scenarios involving suspicious email, lost devices, accidental disclosure, and unauthorised access |
| **Communication** | Dedicated reporting-address or reporting-button reminder |
| **Measurement** | Reporting volume, report quality, and time-to-report compared with baseline |
| **Deliverable** | Incident-reporting awareness summary |

**Expected behaviour:** Employees report quickly, including after an honest mistake, without attempting to hide the incident.

---

## October: Third Phishing Simulation and Q3 Review

**Theme:** Validate behavioural improvement ahead of year-end evaluation.

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| **Primary audience** | All employees |
| **Owner** | Security Manager |
| **Activities** | Controlled payment-change or account-alert simulation; role-specific feedback; Q3 programme review |
| **Communication** | Targeted reminders based on observed weaknesses |
| **Measurement** | Click and reporting rates compared with all earlier simulations |
| **Deliverable** | Q3 performance report and final-quarter action plan |

**Governance checkpoint:** Escalate unresolved resource, participation, or control gaps to Executive Management.

---

## November: Refresher Training and Employee Feedback

**Theme:** Reinforce weak areas and improve programme accessibility.

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| **Primary audience** | All employees |
| **Owner** | HR Manager and Security Manager |
| **Activities** | Refresher training; short questionnaire; anonymous feedback; departmental discussions; warehouse and remote-sales feedback sessions |
| **Communication** | Year-in-review awareness message |
| **Measurement** | Knowledge scores, completion rates, feedback themes, and unresolved learning gaps |
| **Deliverable** | Refresher and employee-feedback report |

**Improvement checkpoint:** Update content for different roles, shifts, locations, and levels of technical understanding.

---

## December: Final Simulation and Annual Evaluation

**Theme:** Measure outcomes and prepare the next programme cycle.

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| **Primary audience** | All employees and Leadership |
| **Owner** | Security Manager |
| **Activities** | Final phishing simulation; annual performance analysis; leadership presentation; improvement planning; employee recognition |
| **Communication** | Programme results and security-recognition message |
| **Measurement** | Year-end phishing reduction, MFA adoption, reporting increase, completion rates, and knowledge-score improvement |
| **Deliverable** | Annual Security Awareness Programme Report and next-year improvement plan |

**Year-end decision:** Approve revised objectives, content priorities, owners, and resources for the next 12-month cycle.

## Quarterly Review Checklist

- [ ] Compare current results with the Month 1 baseline.
- [ ] Review phishing click, submission, and reporting rates.
- [ ] Review MFA adoption and outstanding eligible accounts.
- [ ] Review training completion and knowledge scores.
- [ ] Examine incident trends without treating increased reporting as automatic evidence of increased incidents.
- [ ] Review employee feedback and accessibility concerns.
- [ ] Identify emerging threats and role-specific knowledge gaps.
- [ ] Assign corrective actions to named owners.
- [ ] Set target dates and track actions to completion.
- [ ] Escalate material resource or governance issues to Executive Management.
